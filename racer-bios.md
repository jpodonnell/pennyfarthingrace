## Christopher Koch — Frederick, MD
**Submitted:** 2026-04-07

- Years riding: I started riding in late April 2023, less than 3 months before my first race.
- Bike brand: Standard Highwheel / Racer
- Wheel size: 54"
- Origin story: After watching the National Clustered Spires Highwheel Race in Frederick, MD, several times over the years, I took a lesson from another highwheeler. I was instantly hooked and bought a Rideable Bike Replica (RBR) Excelsior Standard 52" the next day! After three races with a heavier leisure-intended bike, I sought out a Standard High Wheel Racer and upped to 54". I ride both bikes regularly depending the type of outing.
- Fun fact: I thoroughly enjoy celebrating my German heritage through travel to Oktoberfest in Munich and similar local festivals. I was once dubbed the "Bavarian Doughboy" for my love of pretzels and German bier. Where as many of the folks wear old timey attire in races, I've often sported my lederhosen.

## Mark Pope — New Ellenton, SC
**Submitted:** 2026-04-10

- Years riding: 8
- Bike brand: Standard High Wheel
- Wheel size: 50"
- Origin story: I rediscovered cycling as an adult in the Spring of 2007 while working at Kennedy Space Center. In the years following, I would occasionally see YouTube videos of people riding Penny Farthings and would think, "Wow, that looks like fun!" But it would be 2018 before I got up the courage to order my bicycle from Per-Olaf Kippel in Sweden. Then, in 2022, I made my first race in Frederick, Maryland. And haven't looked back.
- Fun fact: Well, it wasn't fun at the time, but four days after bringing home my bike I messed up a dismount and busted two of my right ribs. Two weeks later I was back in the saddle and thought it would be funny to name my penny, Tumbleweed. Guess the bike didn't like the name because three weeks after that it threw me the other way and I broke my left femur. Ouch! But from the start my wife, Karin, had called the bike "Speedy," a little pretentious but safer name.

## Shayne Boucher — Frederick, MD
**Submitted:** 2026-04-15

- Years riding: 8 years, since 2018 when I first started racing high wheels at the National Clustered Spires High Wheel Race
- Bike brand: Standard Highwheels Racer / Pastel Blue
- Wheel size: 52"
- Origin story: I got into penny farthing riding after watching the exhilarating National Clustered Spires High Wheel Races, which immediately convinced me I had to try it myself. In 2018, after a quick lesson from legendary racer Larry Black, I was riding a high wheel on public roads, and just three months later I showed up at the starting line on my own 48" black Standard Highwheels Tourer. What began as a thrill quickly became a passion, drawing me into a rare, welcoming global community of adventurous riders, and eventually leading my wife and me to race and ride in places like Sweden, Tasmania, and across the US — turning high wheel riding into an unforgettable way of life rather than just a hobby.
- Fun fact: A fun fact about me is that when I'm not working as a staff research scientist, I teach riding high wheel bicycles and I've even ridden through the historic streets of Old San Juan, Puerto Rico along with premier international racer Fidel Irizarry to help raise funds for a local cancer treatment center.
