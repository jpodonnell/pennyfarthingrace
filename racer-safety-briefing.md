# Racer Safety Briefing — Ohio High Wheel Race

Read aloud to all racers before the start of the race.

1. **Faster riders stay to the inside.**

2. **Signal "single" before passing** to let the rider ahead know you're coming through. Passing is always on the right. If you're the one being passed, make room and don't hug the inside line.

3. **This is a friendly race.** Do not bump, touch, or interact with other racers. If we see it happening, you will be asked to leave the race.

4. **Lapped riders:** since this is a race to zero, faster riders will lap you. Hold your line and stay aware when someone calls out to pass.

5. **Listen to the MC/DJ** for any announcements during the race.

6. **This is a race to zero.** If there's time on the clock, keep going unless told otherwise.

7. **Be mindful of spectators.** None of them should be on the course, but please watch out for them anyway.

8. **Grate at the last turn.** There's a grate in the street with cones on and around it. Be mindful and careful around it.

9. **Water at the third turn.** Volunteers will be handing out cups of water if needed. We have plenty, so stay hydrated. This is an hour long race, not a sprint.

10. **If you need to take a break, take a break.**

11. **If you need to stop for good,** exit the course and walk back outside of the course to the start/finish line and let us know.

12. **Helmets required, no exceptions.** If you're not wearing one, you will be told to leave the race.

13. **Tools are available at the start/finish line** if you need to fix or work on your bike.

14. **No headphones, earbuds, or phones while racing.** Resist the urge for a selfie or an "ussie." No phones, period.

15. **Bibs must be visible** to the people tracking laps and time. Pin it to the left side of your jersey or shirt for best visibility, or wear it on your back as a precaution.

16. **We are all starting at the same time.** The DJ/MC will count us down. This is a race for fun. You are competing for a free entry into next year's race.

17. **There will be a top male racer and a top female racer.**
